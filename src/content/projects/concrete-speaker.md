  <h2 class="font-display text-2xl font-bold text-[var(--text-primary)] !my-0">The Idea</h2>
</div>

<p class="text-base leading-relaxed opacity-90">
  I wanted desktop speakers. I also needed a monitor stand to raise my screen to eye level. And I wanted something on my desk that felt intentionally designed, not bought off a shelf and forgotten.
</p>

<p class="text-base leading-relaxed opacity-90">
  Most products only solved one of these, which cluttered up the desk. So the question was: <strong>what if the speakers, the monitor stand, and the desk centerpiece were all one object?</strong>
</p>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-3 gap-4 my-6">
  <div class="p-4 border border-[var(--border)] bg-[var(--bg-surface)] rounded flex flex-col justify-between">
    <div class="font-mono text-xs uppercase text-[var(--amber)] font-bold mb-2">01. Ergonomics</div>
    <div class="font-display font-semibold text-[var(--text-primary)] text-sm mb-1">Eye-Level Stand</div>
    <p class="text-xs opacity-80 leading-relaxed">Raises the monitor to eye level and keeps open space underneath for small desk items.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="p-4 border border-[var(--border)] bg-[var(--bg-surface)] rounded flex flex-col justify-between">
    <div class="font-mono text-xs uppercase text-[var(--royal)] font-bold mb-2">02. Sound</div>
    <div class="font-display font-semibold text-[var(--text-primary)] text-sm mb-1">Angled Drivers</div>
    <p class="text-xs opacity-80 leading-relaxed">Slants the front face upward toward my ears to reduce sound bouncing off the desk.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="p-4 border border-[var(--border)] bg-[var(--bg-surface)] rounded flex flex-col justify-between">
    <div class="font-mono text-xs uppercase text-[var(--crimson)] font-bold mb-2">03. Materials</div>
    <div class="font-display font-semibold text-[var(--text-primary)] text-sm mb-1">Concrete & Wood</div>
    <p class="text-xs opacity-80 leading-relaxed">Solid concrete paired with warm hardwood veneer instead of thin plastic.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="p-4 rounded border-l-4 border-l-[var(--amber)] bg-[var(--bg-surface)] border border-[var(--border)] my-6">
  <div class="font-mono text-xs text-[var(--amber)] font-bold uppercase mb-1">The Constraint</div>
  <p class="text-sm opacity-90 mb-0">
    No workshop, no power tools, and no 3D printer. The entire project had to be built with accessible materials, a utility knife, and hand-poured concrete.
  </p>
</div>

  <h2 class="font-display text-2xl font-bold text-[var(--text-primary)] !my-0">Why Concrete & Wood</h2>
</div>

<p class="text-base leading-relaxed opacity-90">
  The materials were picked for practical reasons, not just how they look:
</p>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-3 gap-6 my-6">
  <div class="p-5 border border-[var(--border)] bg-[var(--bg-surface)] rounded-lg space-y-2">
    <div class="font-mono text-xs uppercase text-[var(--amber)] font-bold">Housing</div>
    <h3 class="font-display text-lg font-bold text-[var(--text-primary)] !mt-0 !mb-1">Cast Concrete</h3>
    <p class="text-xs md:text-sm opacity-85 leading-relaxed">
      Heavy and dense. The weight stops the speaker boxes from vibrating, preventing the hollow resonance common in cheap plastic speaker boxes.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="p-5 border border-[var(--border)] bg-[var(--bg-surface)] rounded-lg space-y-2">
    <div class="font-mono text-xs uppercase text-[var(--royal)] font-bold">Front Panel</div>
    <h3 class="font-display text-lg font-bold text-[var(--text-primary)] !mt-0 !mb-1">Hardwood Veneer</h3>
    <p class="text-xs md:text-sm opacity-85 leading-relaxed">
      Used for the front baffle. It is easy to cut cleanly for the drivers and adds a warm look next to the raw gray concrete.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="p-5 border border-[var(--border)] bg-[var(--bg-surface)] rounded-lg space-y-2">
    <div class="font-mono text-xs uppercase text-[var(--crimson)] font-bold">Inside Lining</div>
    <h3 class="font-display text-lg font-bold text-[var(--text-primary)] !mt-0 !mb-1">Cotton Batting</h3>
    <p class="text-xs md:text-sm opacity-85 leading-relaxed">
      A simple layer of cotton batting padded inside the boxes to absorb internal sound bounces and keep the vocals clear.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

  <h2 class="font-display text-2xl font-bold text-[var(--text-primary)] !my-0">What Worked and What Didn't</h2>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 my-6">

  <!-- Worked -->
  <div class="p-5 border border-emerald-900/50 bg-[var(--bg-surface)] rounded-lg space-y-3">
    <div class="font-mono text-xs uppercase text-emerald-400 font-bold flex items-center gap-1.5">
      <span>✓</span> What Worked
    </div>
    <ul class="space-y-2 text-sm opacity-90">
      <li><strong>All-in-one setup:</strong> Having the monitor stand and speakers as one unit cleared up desk space and hidden cables.</li>
      <li><strong>No desk vibration:</strong> The heavy concrete boxes stay completely still even at higher volumes.</li>
      <li><strong>Clean sound:</strong> Cotton lining helps avoid a hollow, boxy sound in the vocals.</li>
      <li><strong>Daily reliability:</strong> It sits on my desk and works well every day.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <!-- Didn't Work -->
  <div class="p-5 border border-[var(--crimson)]/40 bg-[var(--bg-surface)] rounded-lg space-y-3">
    <div class="font-mono text-xs uppercase text-[var(--crimson)] font-bold flex items-center gap-1.5">
      <span>✕</span> What Didn't & Honest Gaps
    </div>
    <ul class="space-y-2 text-sm opacity-90">
      <li><strong>Unfinished drawer:</strong> I planned a sliding storage drawer under the bridge, but ran out of time and tools to make it cleanly.</li>
      <li><strong>Box volume by eye:</strong> The internal box size was estimated to fit my desk rather than calculated from the driver specs.</li>
      <li><strong>Mold seam marks:</strong> Handmade foam molds flexed slightly under wet concrete, leaving visible seam lines.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

</div>

<div class="border border-[var(--border)] bg-[var(--bg-surface)] p-5 rounded-lg my-6">
  <p class="text-sm md:text-base leading-relaxed opacity-90 mb-4">
    Things I would do differently on a second version:
  </p>
  <div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-3 font-mono text-xs">
    <div class="p-3 border border-[var(--border)] bg-[var(--bg)] rounded flex items-start gap-2">
      <span class="text-[var(--amber)] font-bold">01</span>
      <div>
        <strong class="text-[var(--text-primary)] block">Custom Bluetooth Amp Board:</strong>
        Build a dedicated amplifier board with Bluetooth and a rotary volume knob.
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="p-3 border border-[var(--border)] bg-[var(--bg)] rounded flex items-start gap-2">
      <span class="text-[var(--royal)] font-bold">02</span>
      <div>
        <strong class="text-[var(--text-primary)] block">Tuned Box Volume:</strong>
        Calculate the exact air volume and add a tuned bass port for better low end.
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="p-3 border border-[var(--border)] bg-[var(--bg)] rounded flex items-start gap-2">
      <span class="text-[var(--crimson)] font-bold">03</span>
      <div>
        <strong class="text-[var(--text-primary)] block">Cleaner Molds:</strong>
        Use laser-cut or CNC molds for sharper edges and even wall thickness.
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="p-3 border border-[var(--border)] bg-[var(--bg)] rounded flex items-start gap-2">
      <span class="text-[var(--amber)] font-bold">04</span>
      <div>
        <strong class="text-[var(--text-primary)] block">Built-in USB-C Power:</strong>
